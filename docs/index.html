Fly Brain Explorer

A whole-brain spiking model of the adult fruit fly, wired from the FlyWire connectome (~139,000 neurons, ~5 million synapses). Pick an experiment: a few neurons are stimulated, and you can watch the activity spread through the rest of the brain.

Brain map

Front view. Grey dots are a sample of all neurons; coloured dots are neurons that fired.

Spike raster

Each row is one active neuron, sorted by when it first fired. Each dot is one spike.

Active neurons

Click a column to sort. IDs open the neuron in FlyWire Codex.

Model: leaky integrate-and-fire neurons with alpha synapses (Shiu et al., bioRxiv 2023), run with the PyTorch backend of eonsystemspbc/fly-brain. Connectome and cell annotations: FlyWire v783 (annotations).

Results are precomputed. Simulating 1 s of brain activity takes about 5 minutes on a laptop CPU, so this page plays back saved runs rather than simulating live.
